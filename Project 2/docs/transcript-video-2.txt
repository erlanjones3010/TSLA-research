[0:29] The config file holds my Lab 13 mandate. Each rule is tagged with its Lab
13 statement. The 6.5% objective is S2, the 3% reserve is S3, long-only is S5,

[0:55] At the bottom is the driver panel. These eight drivers are exposed. The
committee can move them, but only inside these bounds, and each one has a
reason. The equity premium, for example, goes from 0 to 8%. The locked list
holds what the mandate fixes, like the issuer limit and the liquidity rule.

[1:19] Going up to this file, dgs10_asof: the signal is the 10-year Treasury
yield from ALFRED, as it was known on each date. Each observation is dated
before its decision date, so the model never uses information it wouldn't have
had, and the tests check that.

[1:39] Now let's go to the backbone, the engine. signal_on gets the yield known
on the decision date and flags it if it's stale. expected_returns turns that
yield into expected returns: TLT earns the yield, VCIT adds a credit spread, and
SPY adds the equity premium. covariance_matrix estimates risk from the last 24
months. constrained is the optimizer: it tries every mix in 5% steps, keeps the
ones that meet every limit and reach 6.5%, and picks the lowest risk. backtest
rebalances once a year with drift and trading costs. mandate_checks shows pass
or fail with the rule broken. The stress run and stop condition rerun 8
scenarios and stop the process if two fail. And lastly, erp_sweep reruns the
portfolio at equity premiums from 2% to 6%.

[2:52] Going over to the app and reset_to_base: app.py builds the driver panel,
and this reset_to_base function puts every driver back to its base value.

[3:19] Now let's run some code. I go to run_analysis and run it. run_analysis.py
runs the same analysis without the app and saves it. The output shows the
inputs, the as-of date, the verdicts, the stress run, the stop condition and the
equity premium sweep.

[3:49] Now I run the tests. All 12 tests pass. My highest-risk claim is that
there's no look-ahead: this test changes every price after a decision date and
checks that the decision doesn't change. The monotonicity test checks that a
higher equity premium never needs more SPY, and a known-answer test confirms the
3% reserve matches my Lab 13 checker.

[4:26] Known limitations: one limitation is that the backtest only covers 2023
